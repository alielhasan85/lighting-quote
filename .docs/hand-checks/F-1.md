# F-1 — check by hand

**Run:** `npm run dev` · open http://localhost:3000/login · an inbox you can open. Needs: the founder actions in
NOW.md (Neon dev branch, Google OAuth, Resend key, `EMAIL_FROM`, `AUTH_SECRET`, `CRON_SECRET`) and the `rate_limit`
migration applied.

Every magic link you send counts against the limits: 3 per email per 15 minutes, 10 per IP per hour. Walk the steps
in order, and use a fresh address where a step says so.

1. **The login page (6).** Signed out.
   - Open `/login` → the product name at the top, an "Email" field, a "Send sign-in link" button, an "Or" divider and
     a "Continue with Google" button. No Apple button, no password field, no "Sign up" link.

2. **Email sign-in (7).** Signed out. This uses 1 of your 3 links.
   - Enter your email → "Send sign-in link" → you land on `/login/check-email`: "Check your email" and "We sent a
     sign-in link. It expires in 15 minutes."
   - Open the email and click the link → you're signed in and land on `/`.
   - Send yourself another link and click it twice → the second click lands on `/login` with "That sign-in link has
     expired or was already used. Request a new one." (Skip this if you want to keep your 3 links for step 4.)

3. **Already signed in (9).** Still signed in from step 2.
   - Open `/login` → you're sent straight to `/`.

4. **Where you land after sign-in (10).** Sign out first at `/api/auth/signout`. This uses 2 links.
   - Open `/login?callbackUrl=/w/test`, sign in by email → the address bar shows `/w/test` (a 404 page until F-2
     builds workspaces: the address is what's being checked).
   - Sign out, open `/login?callbackUrl=https://evil.com`, sign in → you land on `/`, never on evil.com.

5. **Rate limit (8).** Signed out. Use a **fresh** address, e.g. `you+limit@yourdomain.com`.
   - Send a link for it 3 times → each lands on "Check your email".
   - Send a 4th time → back on `/login` with "Too many sign-in links. Try again in 15 minutes." No 4th email arrives.

6. **Forbidden page (11).**
   - Open `/forbidden` → "You don't have access to this page", "Ask a workspace owner to invite you, or sign in with
     another account.", and two buttons: "Go to home" (goes to `/`) and "Sign in with another account" (goes to the
     sign-out page).

7. **Security headers (12).** In a second terminal:
   - `curl -sI http://localhost:3000/login` → the response lists `Strict-Transport-Security`,
     `X-Content-Type-Options: nosniff`, `Referrer-Policy: strict-origin-when-cross-origin`, `Permissions-Policy` and
     `Content-Security-Policy: frame-ancestors 'self'`.

8. **Nightly cleanup (13).**
   - `curl -i http://localhost:3000/api/cron/auth-cleanup` → `401`.
   - `curl -i -H "Authorization: Bearer <your CRON_SECRET>" http://localhost:3000/api/cron/auth-cleanup` → `200` and
     `{"sessions":…,"tokens":…,"rateLimits":…}` with numbers (0 is fine).

9. **Missing email sender (14).** Stop `npm run dev`. In `.env`, blank the value of `EMAIL_FROM`.
   - Run `npm run dev` → the console warns that `EMAIL_FROM` is missing.
   - Put `EMAIL_FROM` back.

10. **Done when (15).**
    - Open `.docs/references/AUTH_REFERENCE.md` → the Known issues section is empty.
